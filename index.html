    Frequently asked questions

    Delivery, payment, freshness and authenticity — answered.

    Still need help? Contact us
    How fast is delivery?

    Same-day or next-day in Karachi and Lahore. Other cities take 2–4 working days by courier.

    Which payment methods do you accept?

    Cash on delivery in Karachi and Lahore. For all other cities, 100% advance payment is required to confirm the order.

    How long does the coconut water stay fresh?

    Keep the coconut refrigerated and enjoy within 5–7 days of delivery. Once opened, finish it the same day.

    Are your saffron and Madina dates authentic?

    Yes. Our Zafran (Kesar) is tested for purity and our Ajwa (عجوہ), Sukri (سکری) and Mabroom (مبروم) dates are imported directly from Madina with sealed packaging.

    What if an item arrives damaged?
