Product information

Product description

Coco Bliss coconuts are young green coconuts selected for a high water content and a naturally sweet, light taste. Each coconut is trimmed, cleaned and fitted with a smart easy-opener so you can drink straight from the shell — no knife, no spillage.

Nothing is added: no sugar, no flavouring, no concentrate and no preservatives. What you drink is exactly what is inside the coconut.

Ingredients: 100% fresh young coconut water (inside the natural shell).

Benefits & usage
  • A refreshing, naturally light drink — great after a workout or in summer heat.
  • Drink chilled straight from the shell using the easy-opener and the supplied straw.
  • Use the water in smoothies, lemonades or chia drinks.
  • Scoop the soft malai (flesh) with a spoon once the water is finished.

Storage & shelf life
  • Keep refrigerated at 2–6 °C from the moment it arrives.
  • Best consumed within 5–7 days of delivery (indicative — confirm with your own testing).
  • Once opened, finish the same day. Do not store an opened coconut.
  • Keep away from direct sunlight and strong-smelling foods.
